## What is ClosingFox?

[ClosingFox](https://closingfox.com/) eliminates the chaos of managing real estate leads across multiple portals. It auto-captures leads from 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, Facebook Ads, and Google Ads - assigning them to your agents instantly.

No spreadsheets. No missed follow-ups. No leads slipping through the cracks.

## Key Features

- **Auto Lead Capture** - 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, Facebook & Google Ads
- **5-Second Assignment** - Round-robin, escalation, broadcast & weighted modes
- **Spot System** - 39 automatic rules catch overdue follow-ups, cherry-picking & fake calls
- **GPS Site Visit Tracking** - Location-verified proof of visits
- **AI Sales Coaching** - Fox Focus helps agents improve conversions
- **3-Tap Call Disposal** - Log calls in seconds, not minutes
- **Offline Mode** - Works without internet on Android
- **WhatsApp Integration** - Communicate with clients directly
- **Pipeline Kanban Board** - Visual deal tracking from lead to close
